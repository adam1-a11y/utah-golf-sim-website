The process

From first visit to first swing.

Six steps, one point of contact. You'll know exactly what you're getting — and what it costs — before we order a single component.

On-site consultation

We come to your space — home or business, anywhere in Utah County, Salt Lake County, Wasatch County, or Summit County. We measure ceiling height, depth, width, and swing clearance, look at lighting and power, and talk through how you'll actually use the room. Free, no obligation, and you get honest advice about what your space can and can't do.

3D room render

Your actual room, rendered with the build in place — enclosure, screen, hitting area, seating, sightlines. This is where changes are free. Move the seating, swap the turf layout, reconsider the launch monitor. Nothing is ordered until the render is right.

Written quote

Line by line: launch monitor, enclosure, screen, projector, turf, software, labor. No package guesswork, no "starting at" pricing that changes once we're inside. Two identical tiers price differently depending on the room, and your quote reflects your room.

Order and schedule

A 50% deposit places the equipment order and books your install window. As authorized dealers for Uneekor, TruGolf, SkyTrak, and Carl's Place, we order direct — no third-party markup, no waiting on a middleman.

Professional installation

Framing, enclosure assembly, screen tensioning, projector mounting, turf, and cleanup — handled by vetted crews we coordinate ourselves. Your house gets protected, and the site gets left cleaner than we found it.

On-site dial-in

Calibration until the numbers match the ball, software configured, and a full walkthrough so you know how to run it. We don't leave until you've hit balls and the room does what the render promised.

Timeline

Measured to playable in weeks, not months.

Consultation and render usually land within the first week. From deposit, most builds are playable inside three to six weeks depending on equipment lead times and how much construction the room needs.
