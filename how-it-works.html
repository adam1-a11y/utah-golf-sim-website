The process

From first visit to first swing.

Six steps, one point of contact — and you know what it costs before anything is ordered.

On-site consultation

We come to your space — home or business, anywhere in Utah County, Salt Lake County, Wasatch County, or Summit County. We measure, check lighting and power, and give you an honest answer about what the room can and can't do. Free, no obligation.

3D room render

Your actual room, rendered with the build in place. This is where changes are free — nothing is ordered until the render is right. You can see examples in the gallery.

Written quote

Line by line — equipment, materials, labor. No "starting at" pricing that changes once we're inside; your quote reflects your room.

Order and schedule

A 50% deposit places the equipment order and books your install window. As authorized dealers we order direct — no middleman markup.

Professional installation

Framing, enclosure, screen tensioning, projector, turf, cleanup — vetted crews we coordinate ourselves, and the site is left cleaner than we found it.

On-site dial-in

Calibration, software, and a walkthrough. We don't leave until you've hit balls and the room does what the render promised.

Timeline

Measured to playable in weeks, not months.

Consultation and render inside the first week; from deposit, most builds are playable in three to six weeks depending on equipment lead times and construction.
