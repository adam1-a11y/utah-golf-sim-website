The approach

Designed for your room, not off a shelf.

We don't run a showroom, and that's deliberate. Ceiling height, room depth, lighting, layout — every space is different, so we start where your simulator will actually live. Homes, simulator venues, country clubs, offices, and new construction get the same process, from Provo and Lehi through Salt Lake City to Heber City and Park City.

On-site consultation

We measure your space, talk through how you'll actually use it, and recommend the launch monitor and build that fit. No pressure, no demo-room upsell, no quote written from a phone call.

3D room renders

Before anything is ordered, you see your exact room rendered in 3D — enclosure, screen, hitting area, seating. You approve the room before it exists, so install day holds no surprises.

Professional installation

Framing, enclosure, projector mounting, calibration, cleanup — handled end to end by vetted crews we coordinate ourselves. You get one point of contact from first measurement to first swing.

Three ways to build

Pick the room. We'll tune the rest.

These are starting points, not packages. Every build is quoted to your room, your game, and your budget.
