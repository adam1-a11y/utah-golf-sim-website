Designed for your room, not off a shelf.

We don't run a showroom, and that's deliberate. Every space is different — ceiling height, room depth, lighting, layout. So we start where your simulator will actually live — your home, or your business. Simulator venues, country clubs, offices, and new-construction projects get the same design-first process.

In-home consultation

We measure your space, talk through how you'll use it, and recommend the launch monitor and build that fit — no pressure, no demo-room upsell.

3D room renders

Before anything is ordered, you see your exact room rendered in 3D — enclosure, screen, hitting area, seating — so there are no surprises on install day.

Professional installation

Installs are handled by experienced, vetted installation crews we coordinate end to end — framing, enclosure, projector mounting, calibration, and cleanup.
