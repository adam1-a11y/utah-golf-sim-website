About

A local dealer, not a big-box kit.

Utah Golf Sim LLC brings full-service golf simulator design and installation to homes across Utah County and Salt Lake County.

Why we started this.

Buying a golf simulator shouldn't mean piecing together a launch monitor from one site, a screen from another, and a YouTube playlist on how to hang it all — then hoping your ceiling is tall enough. And it shouldn't mean driving to a warehouse showroom to hit balls in a room that looks nothing like yours.

Utah Golf Sim exists to close that gap. We're an authorized dealer for Uneekor, TruGolf, SkyTrak, and Carl's Place, which means we can spec across the brands that lead this industry and stand behind the result. We design around your actual room with an in-home consultation and a 3D render, then coordinate professional installation through experienced, vetted install crews.

One point of contact from first measurement to first swing — that's the whole model.

Who you'll work with.

Utah Golf Sim is run by Adam Cole Johnston. Adam handles every consultation, every design, and every quote personally. When you call, you're talking to the person who measured your room and drew your render — not a sales queue.

Where we work.

We serve Utah County and Salt Lake County — Provo to Salt Lake City and everywhere between: Lehi, American Fork, Orem, Springville, Draper, Sandy, South Jordan, Herriman, Holladay, and the rest. Just outside those lines? Ask — we're flexible for the right project.
