About

One person is accountable for your build. That person is me.

I'm Adam Johnston. I own Utah Golf Sim, I run the consultation, I coordinate the install, and I'm the number you call if something isn't right.

I spent five years in contracting and handyman work before this. That's where I learned the thing that actually decides whether a job goes well: not the products you install, but whether you show up when you said you would, keep the site clean, and tell people the truth about what their space can do.

I was the most expensive guy in my market. I told clients that directly. They hired me anyway, because they'd rather pay more once than pay less twice.

Golf simulators are the same job with better toys. A simulator is a construction project wearing a technology costume — framing, mounting, sightlines, power, acoustics, and then a launch monitor that has to be calibrated until the numbers match the ball. Most of it is craft. Some of it is math. None of it is something you want to discover halfway through.

Utah Golf Sim is an authorized dealer for Uneekor, TruGolf, SkyTrak, and Carl's Place. That's deliberate. Single-brand installers recommend whatever they sell. I'd rather spec your room against four options and tell you which one actually fits your ceiling, your budget, and how seriously you play.

We started in July 2026. That means you'd be an early client, and I'm not going to dress that up — there's no photo gallery of a hundred finished basements yet. What there is: a properly formed and insured Utah business, direct dealer relationships, real contracting experience, and a very strong incentive to make the first builds the ones people talk about.

How we work

Four things we don't compromise on.

We answer

Same day, not "within 48 hours." If you fill out the quote form in the morning, you hear from a person that afternoon.

We show up

On site, with a tape measure, before anyone talks price. You can't quote a simulator room you haven't stood in.

We tell you no

If your ceiling is too low or your budget won't reach the build you want, you'll hear it at the consultation — not after the deposit clears.

We clean up

The site gets left cleaner than we found it. That's not a slogan, it's the part of contracting most people get wrong and everyone remembers.
