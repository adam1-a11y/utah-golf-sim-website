About

One person is accountable for your build. That person is me.

I'm Adam Johnston. I own Utah Golf Sim, I run the consultation, I coordinate the install, and I'm the number you call if something isn't right.

I spent five years in contracting before this. That's where I learned what actually decides whether a job goes well: show up when you said you would, keep the site clean, and tell people the truth about what their space can do.

I was the most expensive guy in my market. I told clients that directly. They hired me anyway, because they'd rather pay more once than pay less twice.

A simulator is a construction project wearing a technology costume — framing, sightlines, power, acoustics, then a launch monitor calibrated until the numbers match the ball. None of it is something you want to discover halfway through.

Utah Golf Sim builds with Uneekor, TruGolf, SkyTrak, and Carl's Place — deliberately. Single-brand installers recommend whatever they sell. I'd rather spec your room against four options and tell you which one fits.

We started in July 2026, so you'd be an early client — I won't dress that up. What you get: an insured Utah business, real contracting experience, and a very strong incentive to make the first builds the ones people talk about.

Where we work

Provo to Park City.

We cover four counties across the Wasatch Front and Back. In Utah County and Salt Lake County that means Provo, Orem, Springville, Lehi, American Fork, Draper, Sandy, South Jordan, Herriman, Holladay, and Salt Lake City itself.

Over the mountain, Summit County and Wasatch County are where indoor golf earns its keep: Park City, Kimball Junction, Promontory, Midway, and Heber City. Courses up there close for a good chunk of the year, ski houses tend to have the ceiling height a full swing actually needs, and a simulator room is the difference between a six-month golf season and a twelve-month one.

Just outside those lines? Ask. For the right project we'll make the drive.
